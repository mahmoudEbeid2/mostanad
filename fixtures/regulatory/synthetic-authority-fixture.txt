TITLE: Fictional Veterinary and Animal Feed Product Labeling Guide, Edition 1 (Synthetic)
AUTHORITY: Fictional Animal Health Authority (FAHA)
COUNTRY: Fictionland

PREAMBLE. This guide sets out the labeling requirements for veterinary drugs
and animal feed products marketed in Fictionland. It does not itself impose
requirements and exists only to introduce the sections that follow.

SECTION 1 — PRODUCT IDENTITY
1.1 Every label must state the product name in both Arabic and English.
1.2 Every label must list all active ingredients with their exact amount and
    unit of measure. An ingredient listed without a stated amount is not
    permitted; where no numeric quantity applies, the label must state "q.s."

SECTION 2 — TARGET SPECIES AND WITHDRAWAL
2.1 Every label must state the target animal species for which the product is
    intended.
2.2 If the product's target animal species includes Cattle, Sheep, Goat, or
    Poultry, the label must state a withdrawal period in days before the
    animal or its products (meat, milk, eggs) may enter the food supply.
2.3 Products intended solely for companion animals (Dogs, Cats) are exempt
    from the withdrawal period requirement in clause 2.2.

SECTION 3 — PROHIBITED CLAIMS
3.1 A label must not claim that the product "cures" or "treats" any disease
    unless the product is registered as a veterinary drug with a valid
    registration number. Feed additives and supplements must not use the
    words "cures", "treats", "heals", or the Arabic equivalent "يعالج".
3.2 A label must not state or imply that the product is safe for human
    consumption.

SECTION 4 — STORAGE AND HANDLING
4.1 Every label must state a storage temperature limit expressed in degrees
    Celsius. A vague instruction such as "store in a cool place" without a
    numeric limit does not satisfy this requirement.
4.2 Products packaged in a multi-dose container (bottle, vial, or drum) must
    state the shelf life after first opening.

SECTION 5 — MANUFACTURER AND REGISTRATION
5.1 Every label must display the name of the manufacturer or importer.
5.2 Veterinary drug products must display a valid registration number issued
    by FAHA. Feed additives and supplements are exempt from this requirement.
5.3 Every label must display a batch number and an expiry date.

SECTION 6 — LANGUAGE
6.1 All mandatory statements under this guide must appear in both Arabic and
    English on the same label. A label in English only, or Arabic only, does
    not satisfy this guide.

SECTION 7 — CHILD SAFETY
7.1 Every label must display a statement directing that the product be kept
    out of the reach of children.

APPENDIX A — DEFINITIONS (informational, not a requirement)
"Withdrawal period" means the minimum time that must elapse between the last
administration of a product and the legal marketing of food derived from the
treated animal.
